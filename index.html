Where most photography stops

Sharp. Well lit. Correctly exposed.

Then we go one level up.

Level 01

Seen

The technical basics, done properly. Focus, light and colour you never have to think about.

Level 02

Felt

Direction and timing, so the person in the frame is actually present and the picture says so.

Level 03

Remembered

A set with a point of view, retouched by hand, that still holds up ten years from now.

How a shoot works

Five steps, no surprises. You always know what happens next.

  1. Brief

    We talk about the look you want, where the photos will be used and who will be in front of the camera.

  2. Shoot

    A half or full day. Natural light first, flash only when the picture needs it.

  3. Select

    You get a private gallery within 48 hours and pick your favourites.

  4. Retouch

    Every chosen photo is retouched by hand, so people still look like themselves.

  5. Deliver

    Final files within a week, ready for print and web.
